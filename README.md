# newMath (`Mathmatica`)

A Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization project exploring **Renormalization Group (RG) Dynamics**, **Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking**, **Feigenbaum Theory**, and their structural bridges to **Computational Complexity ($P \text{ vs } NP$)** and **SAT Proof Complexity**.

All modules are designed following Mathlib conventions (zero `sorry`, zero warnings) with the goal of gradually upstreaming general-purpose theorems (such as the Schwarzian Derivative and Davis–Putnam Resolution Completeness) to `leanprover-community/mathlib4`.

## Refined Conjecture Bridges (Audited Research Roadmap)

1. **Bridge I' — Basis-Dependent Renormalizability**: Renormalizability depends on the effective operator basis $\mathcal{B}$ (e.g., 3-XOR-SAT blows up in the CNF resolution basis, yet has zero-blowup monotone elimination in the $\mathbb{F}_2$-linear basis — formally verified in `XORSATBasis.lean`). A CSP belongs to $P$ iff there exists a poly-time algebraic basis $\mathcal{B}$ in which the RG flow closes inside a polynomial-size effective subspace.
2. **Bridge II' — Depth–Expansion Rate Coupling**: Logarithmic-depth parallel coarse-graining ($D(n) = O(\log n)$) with constant Feigenbaum-like expansion $\delta \le 2^c$ stays polynomial ($n^c$), whereas linear-depth sequential elimination ($D(n) = \Theta(n)$) stays in $P$ only under polynomial/zero-blowup closure ($\delta = 1$, as in `RGTreewidth.lean` and `XORSATBasis.lean`).
3. **Bridge III' — Equivariant RG & Algebraic-Basis Obstructions**: Combining `ExpanderWidth.lean` + `BSWDAGFull.lean` yields unconditional exponential CDCL lower bounds from boundary expansion; extending beyond fixed proof systems to general circuits requires simultaneously ruling out algebraic basis changes and bypassing the Razborov–Rudich Natural Proofs barrier (`ComplexityBarriers.lean`).
4. **Bridge IV' — Negative Schwarzian Rigidity vs. Stable-Solver OGP**: OGP (`OverlapGap.lean` + `OGPBarrier.lean`) sharply characterizes the phase transition of **stable / local dynamical solvers** (and does not rule out non-stable algebraic solvers like Gaussian elimination). Seeking a discrete curvature/Schwarzian-type RG invariant separating stable tractability from OGP shattering remains an active target.
